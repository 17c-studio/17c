Accessibility

Thoughtful websites should be welcoming and usable. 17C Studio aims to make this website easier to use for people with different abilities, devices, and ways of navigating.

Practices on this site

The site includes a skip link, semantic page landmarks, labeled form fields, keyboard controls, visible focus indicators, meaningful image descriptions where appropriate, and support for reduced-motion preferences. Service details, the wishlist, and the Sonder definition can be operated without hover. A control on the homepage can pause decorative motion.

Help accessing information

If anything makes it difficult to use this website, please email marielwhitney.design@gmail.com. If you can, share the page, the difficulty you encountered, and the device or assistive technology you were using. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

You can also request another way to access information or discuss a project by email rather than using the inquiry form or scheduler.

Ongoing work

WCAG 2.2 Level AA is the target for continued improvements. This statement does not claim formal conformance or certification. Third-party forms and scheduling tools may have limitations outside the studio’s direct control.
