Overview

Helping readers find Unblind, without Bailey living on social media.

Ahead of the February 8, 2027 Book 2 launch, we'll help Bailey show up consistently for readers, using one batched filming session a month so her writing time stays protected. I’ll handle the day-to-day social work while Sasquatch leads the campaign and works directly with Bailey.

Responsibilities

Scope I can support

I’ll own

  • Editing short-form video, adapting each video for Instagram (mirrored to Facebook), TikTok, and YouTube Shorts (format, length, captions), and scheduling in Buffer.
  • Suggesting which posts to boost and setting up approved boosts, plus community replies and reshares.
  • Sending Sasquatch monthly metrics plus anecdotal highlights for its report to Bailey.
  • With Package 2, shaping the outreach plan with Sasquatch from its influencer list, then carrying it out, including requests for honest Book 2 reviews supporting Sasquatch’s 50-review campaign goal.

Stays with Sasquatch

  • Campaign strategy, filming, and post and spend approvals. Sasquatch manages the client relationship and routes Bailey’s requests and feedback to me.
  • The influencer list, newsletters, the website and analytics, and character art.
  • Reporting to Bailey.

What I’ll do

Bailey's voice and story, on every platform.

  • At kickoff, I’ll ask Bailey to share a couple of posts she’d be excited to put her name on. We’ll use those to agree on a creative direction before editing the first batch. One idea to explore is a short video about how her legal background shaped a rule or conflict in the Unblind world. Both packages include one kickoff workshop. Additional strategy sessions are billed at an agreed rate.
  • After the first filming session, I’ll try two or three styles on one sample clip. Once Bailey chooses a direction, I’ll use it for the rest of the batch.
  • Turn each month's filming session with Bailey into up to 12 original short videos, with 1 consolidated revision round per batch. I’ll write captions and adapt and schedule those edits in Buffer for up to 12 publishing placements each on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts. Facebook mirrors approved Instagram posts. The 12 core videos become about 36 platform-specific pieces. These monthly caps also apply in 5-week months.
  • I’ll check comments, messages, and mentions every business day, plus once each weekend. I’ll handle routine replies and pass sensitive questions to Sasquatch.
  • With Package 2, I’ll shape the outreach plan with Sasquatch from its influencer list, then carry out personalized pitches, follow-ups, and requests for honest Book 2 reviews supporting Sasquatch’s 50-review campaign goal. Includes up to 20 new reviewers per month, up to 2 follow-ups each, a shared tracker, and monthly results.
  • Each month, I’ll send you a short recap of what went live, social reach, engagement, audience growth, and what readers responded to. I’ll include a recommendation for what to post or boost next. With Package 2, I’ll also share who I contacted, who replied, and who’s interested in a review copy, along with three takeaways and one thing to try next month.
