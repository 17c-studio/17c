How we’ll measure progress

Before the first changes, I’ll save the available historical reports for up to the preceding 90 days, plus the comparable prior-year period where retained data allows. I’ll record the date of each release so later comparisons distinguish the periods before and after changes and account for seasonality. We’ll set numerical targets after reviewing the starting data.

  • Discovery: traffic sources and relevant search queries where connected reporting makes them available, separating nationwide product discovery from local visit, appointment, and event searches.

  • Shopping: add-to-cart activity, checkout activity, completed orders, and online revenue where the available reporting supports them.
  • Appointments and events: booking or inquiry activity where your booking tools support measurement. A website click is different from a completed appointment; neither establishes an in-store sale without an appropriate record.

Reporting review versus setup: reviewing and saving the available existing reports is included in every option. 04 · Questions answered up front

The questions you may be asking.

Are we keeping Shopify and our current theme?

Yes. The starting point is improving your existing Shopify store and theme. I’ll review their capabilities before recommending changes. A new theme, custom theme, or platform move would need a separate discussion and quote.

Will this help online sales or bring people into the store?

The plan supports both, with different paths for each. Clothing collections can serve online shoppers, while formalwear, permanent jewelry, and parties can help people plan a visit or make a booking. The reporting will help us see what is happening without promising a particular sales result.

Can formalwear stay focused on in-store shopping?

Yes. Options 2 and 3 include the agreed page changes that explain your approved final-sale policy and encourage in-store try-ons. Option 1 reviews the current experience and includes recommendations in the action plan. Online formalwear purchases are not the central growth strategy.

What will we still need to manage?

Your team will continue to manage inventory accuracy, orders, fulfillment, customer service, and routine product entry unless specific tasks are separately included. New collection setup is a different task from entering every product. Any product-editing allowance will be listed explicitly.

How will we know whether the changes are helping?

I’ll save the available historical reporting before changes and record release dates, then compare discovery, browsing, shopping, and booking activity with that baseline. We’ll choose numerical targets after reviewing the data. Clicks, completed appointments, online orders, and in-store purchases will be treated as separate measures.

Is new reporting setup included?

The project includes reviewing existing Shopify analytics, available Search Console performance and indexing reports, and existing connected analytics or booking reports. Missing search-reporting connections, new analytics installations, custom event tracking, and booking attribution setup are separately quoted before work begins. I’ll document data gaps rather than assume the information is available.

Does search work guarantee Google rankings or AI recommendations?

No. I can improve the agreed pages and help you understand the available evidence, but I can’t guarantee rankings, AI recommendations, traffic, or sales. I’ll keep the recommendations tied to useful information for your customers and measurable work.

What happens if an app or theme limitation needs extra work?

I’ll explain the limitation, the available alternatives, and any additional cost before proceeding. A new paid app, major feature, custom integration, or theme replacement needs your approval and a separate scope if it falls outside the chosen project.

Is ongoing support part of the project fee?

It’s optional and separate from the project fee. The same service is $300 per month for a six-month initial commitment ($1,800 total) or $250 per month for a twelve-month initial commitment ($3,000 total), billed monthly from launch. You can choose no care plan. The allowance and renewal terms are set out in Investment; care does not mean unlimited updates or taking over every store responsibility.
