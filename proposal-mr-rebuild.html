Questions

A few things worth explaining.

What is WordPress, and why would I use it?

WordPress is software for building and updating a website. In Option 2, I’ll use it to create your custom site, with galleries and service pages organized around your business. You and your mom can edit everyday text and photos after training. “Self-hosted” means it runs with a hosting provider in an account you own. If you prefer to keep Wix, Option 1 works with the site you have.

What is Google Analytics, and what will it tell me?

Google Analytics helps show how people find and use your website, including which pages they visit. It can help us see whether people are looking at kitchens, bathrooms, or other services, so we have something to work from when improving the site. It’s included in every project option. We’ll review it alongside your actual inquiries; website visits alone don’t tell us which jobs you win.

What are search basics or SEO?

SEO means search engine optimization: making your website easier for search engines such as Google to understand. I’ll clarify service wording, page titles, and page organization so your kitchen and bathroom remodeling work is described clearly. That helps connect the site to the services people are looking for. Search positions and inquiry numbers can change, so we’ll monitor them rather than promise a particular ranking.

What is quote-form conversion tracking?

It means measuring whether a visit leads to a completed quote request, where the form supports that tracking. A click on a quote button and a submitted inquiry are different steps. This helps us see where people may be stopping and what to improve. You’ll still receive the inquiry and callback number by email.

What are a domain and hosting, and why do I need them?

Your domain is your website address, such as mrrebuild.com. Hosting is the service that keeps the website available online. Wix brings the website tools and hosting together; a self-hosted WordPress site uses a separate hosting provider. We’ll review your existing domain and subscriptions before changing anything. The accounts stay in your name, and hosting and software are charged at cost with your approval.

Do I need to renew Wix?

Please confirm your renewal date and which subscriptions it covers. We’ll review the timing together before changing hosting or domain settings so the site and quote requests stay live.

Can I keep getting quote requests by email?

Yes. Every option preserves email quote requests. I’ll test that the inquiry and callback number arrive in the email itself, without needing to log into Wix or WordPress. The complimentary Wix phone-field update above is separate from the paid work.

Can my mom keep updating the website?

Yes. The Wix refresh keeps your current editor. The custom website includes training so she can make everyday text and photo updates in WordPress. Your domain and accounts stay in your name.

Would a client portal help my business?

A client portal is a private part of your website where customers can view their quotes, proposals, and project updates in one place. It could save you from repeating updates across email and text. How do you manage those things today? If you already use a tool, we’ll look at what it does well and where it falls short before deciding what a portal should do. The starting estimate becomes a fixed quote only after that scope is agreed.
