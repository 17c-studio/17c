01 · Overview

Help more people find the store and enjoy shopping with you.

Your goal is to grow the store’s presence online and make the website easier to manage. The first step is a practical audit of the Shopify site you already have.

You shared that most customers discover Estella Rose while walking downtown, through word of mouth, or through recommendations on social media. You said online orders usually come from outside the area, including outside Michigan, and you welcome orders anywhere. Those orders are still a small part of the business, and you’d like them to contribute more during the slower winter months.

What I heard you want

Bring more people to the website.

Understand how shoppers across the country find your clothing collections, separately from local searches for store visits, appointments, and parties. You welcome online orders anywhere; the search strategy should reflect that.

Make it easier to explore and shop.

Give visitors clear routes into your collections, useful product information, and confidence about what happens when they buy.

Take some of the website work off your plate.

Separate the improvements I can handle from the everyday inventory work your team will keep doing, with a clear choice between training and ongoing support.

The website should support the whole store. Formalwear deserves a place online so customers can discover it and plan a visit to try it on. Everyday clothing can support online shopping, while permanent jewelry, parties, and events need clear paths toward a visit, booking, or inquiry.

Where I would start looking.

These starting points come from our conversation and the issues discussed during the call. They are not a completed technical audit. I’ll verify them before recommending the final fixes.

01

Navigation that keeps shoppers moving

What came up

Our conversation raised a dropdown that closes while moving toward its submenu. The supplied desktop image also shows a page-not-found message. Both are starting points to verify, rather than proven causes of low sales.

What I’d improve

02

Party information people can read

What came up

You offer a trucker hat bar, a charm bar, and off-site permanent jewelry events, as well as in-store appointments. Much of the party information appears inside graphics. I’ll confirm the exact service names, availability, and booking requirements before finalizing the copy.

What I’d improve

I’ll add approved, readable text alongside the images, clarify the booking steps, and confirm the service names and requirements with you. This supports clarity, accessibility, and search understanding.

03

Confidence to browse and buy

You want unfamiliar shoppers to feel comfortable exploring the store and purchasing. You raised limited reviews and questions about buying final-sale formalwear online. The supplied mobile image provides context for the shopping review; it does not establish a usability defect.

Why is the 12-month rate lower?

The service is the same. A year-long commitment lets me plan my time around Estella Rose, spend less time on renewals, and hold your rate for the whole term. I pass that on as a lower monthly price. The 6-month plan offers a shorter commitment at a slightly higher rate.

What’s included each month

A storefront check

I’ll review navigation, key links, mobile layouts, and the main shopping, appointment, and party pathways.

Technical support

I’ll investigate reported Shopify theme or app issues and handle minor fixes within the update allowance. I’ll coordinate with Shopify or app providers when needed. Larger repairs and custom development are quoted separately.

Two small update requests

Each request covers work requiring up to 30 minutes. Examples include changing a homepage promotion, updating event information, swapping supplied images, or organizing an existing collection using products your team has already entered.

The two-request allowance is shared between requested updates and minor technical fixes. If work would exceed it, I’ll explain the scope and obtain approval for a separate quote before proceeding. Unused requests do not roll over.

One focused search improvement

I’ll improve one existing page or collection’s search title, description, internal links, or supporting copy. This is separate from the two-request allowance and excludes creating a new page or running an ongoing content campaign.

A simple performance summary

I’ll explain available traffic sources, shopping activity, completed online orders, and the next recommended priority. Completed appointments will be reported only where the booking system supports measurement. Clicks are different from bookings and in-store sales.

A quarterly check-in

Every three months, we’ll have one 30-minute conversation about results, seasonal priorities, and upcoming changes.

A response within two business days

This means acknowledgment and next steps. Completion timing depends on the work and any third-party dependencies.
