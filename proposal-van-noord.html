Overview

A clearer picture of the shop.

Show the full range of services. Help local customers find you. Give Van Noord a website of its own.

  • Show what’s available. Lead with TV price matching, delivery and installation, motorized roller shades, and golf simulators. Explain home automation in the context of new builds, when customers and electricians are planning how everything will work together.
  • Make the services easier to find. Use clear service descriptions, page titles, and accurate location and contact details so people searching locally can understand what you offer and how to reach you.
  • Keep Van Noord at the center. Build under the shop’s own name. A brief About section can explain how you work with electricians, including Elite, while making it clear that other contractors can work with Van Noord too.

Project photos and short videos can do a lot of the explaining. The four-TV installation, a set of motorized shades in use, or a golf simulator setup can help someone picture what’s possible in their home. People arriving from social media would have a place to see more, learn about the service, and call or email with a question.

We’ll choose approved projects and service details together. Clear information gives search engines more to work with and helps customers decide whether to get in touch.

Help after the site goes live.

  • I can look after it. With ongoing care, I’d handle the agreed upkeep and content updates. You’d have someone to send new project photos, service changes, or a website question to.
  • Haley can handle basic edits, if she wants to. I can give her a practical handoff so she knows how to update the agreed content. We’d talk with her first and decide which tasks she wants to take on and where you’d still want my help.
