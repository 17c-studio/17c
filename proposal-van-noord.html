Technical questions

What is WordPress, and why would I use it?

WordPress is software for building and updating a website. I’ll use it to create your custom site, with service pages and project examples organized around Van Noord’s work. I can handle ongoing upkeep through your chosen care plan, while Haley or another team member keeps access for occasional edits. Option 3 includes a walkthrough for adding projects. What is Google Analytics, and what will it tell me?

Google Analytics helps show how people find and use your website, including which service pages they visit. Where tracking is set up, we can also see whether they tap to call or submit an inquiry. Every build includes analytics setup and a one-time 60-day check-in. Growth Care and Full Care include a one-page monthly report. We’ll review website activity alongside what you’re hearing in the shop; visits alone don’t tell us which jobs you win.

What are search basics or SEO?

SEO means search engine optimization: making your website easier for search engines such as Google to understand. I’ll clarify service wording, page titles, and page organization so your TV installation, motorized shades, golf simulators, and home automation work are described clearly. A Google Business Profile check helps keep the shop’s name, address, hours, and phone number consistent. Search positions and inquiry numbers can change, so we’ll monitor them rather than promise a particular ranking or recommendation from an AI tool.
