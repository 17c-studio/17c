Draft for business and legal review. Not a finalized policy. Prepared October 15, 2026. The operator identity, hosting provider, provider settings, retention practices, and applicable privacy rights must be confirmed before publication.

Who operates this website

This website presents 17C Studio, an independent web design studio in Holland, Michigan. Contact: marielwhitney.design@gmail.com.

Review required: confirm the legal operator or registered business name and add any legally required contact information.

Information you choose to share

Inquiry forms ask for your name, email address, and project description. Your organization and project wishlist are optional. The information is sent to Formspree for processing. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your inquiry.

We use inquiries to respond, learn about potential projects, and discuss next steps. If a project proceeds, separate service arrangements will apply.

Personalization and browser storage

If you use the business-name editor, your chosen name is saved in this browser’s localStorage so it can personalize later visits. Clearing the name in the editor restores the default text. The wishlist stores selected service IDs in localStorage. You can remove wishlist items individually or clear this website’s browser data.

Personalized prospect links select names and optional meeting details from a public file served with the website. A sessionStorage flag remembers whether the welcome animation has played for that link. These links are not private accounts. Contact form names, email addresses, and messages are not saved to localStorage by this site’s code.

Services that receive requests

Formspree processes inquiry submissions. Google Fonts supplies typefaces; jsDelivr supplies animation and scrolling libraries. Some client logos are requested from external websites. Those services can receive connection information, such as an IP address and request metadata, when your browser requests their resources.

Some personalized pages include an embedded Calendly scheduler. Calendly receives requests when that embed loads and processes any booking details you enter. External portfolio, Instagram, and email links open other services, whose own policies apply.

Review required: confirm Formspree recipients, integrations, spam protection, account retention, and any CAPTCHA provider. Confirm Calendly settings, cookies, and booking data practices. Identify the hosting provider and its request logging and retention. Review the providers’ terms and privacy policies.

Analytics and cookies

No analytics tracker, advertising pixel, or email marketing script was found in this site’s source during this review. The site uses the browser storage described above. Embedded services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own settings and policies.

Review required: check the deployed site and hosting dashboard for analytics, cookies, or integrations not represented in this repository. Determine whether additional notices or choices are needed before those services load.

Retention and security

Review required: document how long inquiries, email correspondence, scheduling records, and provider logs are actually kept, who can access them, and how deletion requests are handled. No retention period has been established in this draft.

The site does not request payment information through its inquiry form. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed secure. Please use the business email to arrange another way to share information if needed.

Your questions and choices

You can choose not to submit a form, clear this site’s browser storage, or contact the studio to discuss personal information you have shared. Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning that information.

Review required: confirm applicable rights, verification procedures, response obligations, and any required appeal or complaint information with qualified advice.
