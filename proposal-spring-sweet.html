Overview

One Spring Sweet. Three places to explore.

You described the shop, bridal loft, and florals as “it's all a part of who we are.” The website can give each a clear place while keeping the warmth of Spring Sweet together on one Shopify site.

Your team hears visitors say, “we didn't know you had bridal.” You also said, “my goal is to grow bridal as much as we can.” Bridal should be easy to see from the first visit, with a welcoming route through the process, designers, and appointments. As your team put it, “They've never been brides before”. Clear information can help someone feel ready to book.

  • See all three sides of Spring Sweet. A simple, photo-led homepage introduces the shop, bridal loft, and florals, with a short welcome and readable footer.
  • Find what’s new in Shop. Option 2 adds a featured collection Holly can rotate, with category browsing inside Shop.
  • Give florals a clear path. Lead visitors to approved existing floral content and the agreed gallery or inquiry destination.
  • Make the next step obvious on a phone. You said, “we are phone creatures.” Visitors need visible labels and clear routes they can use by touch and keyboard.

A simple first choice

Your team asked to “keep it simple for them to decide where they want to go on our website.” We’ll start with the working labels Shop, Bridal, Floral, and About, then agree the final wording and mobile menu behavior together.

The warmth carries through

The homepage direction you shared in the walkthrough will guide this phase. We’ll carry the warm background, useful buttons, and small editorial moments into the pages in your chosen option. We’ll review the photo arrangement, count, crops, and phone layout together before building them.

Questions answered up front

What you might be wondering.

Can we keep everything on Shopify, and will Holly be able to update it?

Yes, this plan keeps the site in your Shopify store. I’ll use editable sections for the agreed updates and show Holly how to make them during a walkthrough with a short PDF guide.

Will the collage feel busy or hide the menu on a phone?

We’ll review desktop and mobile layouts together before committing to the image crop and placement. We’ll keep a readable menu, a curated set of mobile images, and clearly labeled paths to each section.

Can Shop have its own categories while the homepage stays simple?

The plan gives shoppers a clear way to browse categories inside Shop. I’ll test the theme with your actual menus before we choose between an editable category section, secondary navigation, or another approach that’s easy to maintain. This work is included in Options 2 and 3.

What if our new photos take longer?

We can approve direction with temporary images and work in small batches every week or two. We’ll agree on a launch window once we know when the final images, copy, and approvals will be ready.

Can a video fit without slowing the page down?

If you want to add video, I’ll check the format, size, and placement on mobile before we agree to include it. A lightweight still image can serve as the video’s preview.

Can you help us understand what people do on the site?

Yes. We’ll check which data is already collecting. Options 2 and 3 include tracking for up to 8 agreed actions and a plain-English dashboard for search, online sales, and visitor activity. Option 3 adds homepage testing and follow-up reviews at 30, 60, and 90 days. We can report confirmed appointments where the booking provider supports it. Ongoing summaries are available through optional care when measurement is in place.

Can you help us show up for wedding gowns and get fewer calls for dresses we don’t sell?

I can review the website and available search data for sources of confusion, clarify the bridal language, and make relevant updates within the chosen scope. Search visibility changes over time. Option 3 adds Google Ads tracking readiness and a short recommendation on where to start. Campaign setup, management, and ad spend are quoted separately.

Can we simplify the apps and the email signup?

In Options 2 and 3, I’ll review up to 10 apps, remove the ones you approve, and review how your email popup and proposed footer signup could work together. Option 3 also includes recommendations, setup, and testing for up to 3 approved Shopify apps. You’ll see any app fees before you decide.
